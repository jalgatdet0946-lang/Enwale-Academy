About

Enwale Academy is an academy that will be open 2027 in Gambella, Ethiopia and south sudan. It focuses on teaching young children practical, real-world knowledge. The word “Enwale” comes from the Nuer language, meaning “today.” Combined with English words, it represents “Today’s Simple Science Academy.” “Simply” means making learning easy to understand. Our academy was created to teach science to young children in a simple and practical way. We believe that practical learning helps children understand better, think creatively, and become innovators who can improve their lives and help others.
